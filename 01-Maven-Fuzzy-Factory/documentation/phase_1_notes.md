## Section 4 - Website Session Structure

### Objective

Inspect the website_sessions table to identify available traffic attribution fields.

### Columns to Investigate

- utm_source
- utm_campaign
- utm_content
- http_referer

### Observation

The `website_sessions` table contains the necessary traffic attribution fields to analyze customer acquisition and marketing performance.

The following columns are available:

- `utm_source` – Marketing source (e.g., gsearch, bsearch, socialbook).
- `utm_campaign` – Marketing campaign associated with the session.
- `utm_content` – Advertisement or campaign variation.
- `http_referer` – Referring website, when available.

These fields provide the foundation for analyzing traffic sources, campaign performance, customer behavior, and conversion rates in the following sections.

## Section 15 - Identifying the First Pageview

### Business Objective

Identify the first page viewed during each website session in order to determine the landing page.

### SQL Concepts

- GROUP BY
- MIN()
- Aggregate functions

### Observation

Each website session may contain multiple pageviews. By selecting the minimum `website_pageview_id` for each `website_session_id`, we identify the first page viewed during the session. The pageview IDs are sequential across the entire website, so they do not necessarily match the session IDs.

### Business Insight

Identifying the first pageview is the foundation of landing page analysis. Once the first pageview is known, it can be joined back to the `website_pageviews` table to determine which page visitors used to enter the website. This enables further analysis of landing page traffic, conversion rates, and user behavior.

### Future Analysis
